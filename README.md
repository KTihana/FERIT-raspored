# FERIT raspored

Your FERIT timetable on your phone — classes, exams, and personal events in one calendar.

## Your week at a glance

See all seven days on a shared time grid, with colour-coded classes and clear gaps between them. Rotate your phone or choose **Tjedan** for the weekly view; tap a class for its details.

<img src="docs/07-week.jpg" width="900" alt="Weekly calendar with classes, a personal event, and free time between sessions">

## Daily schedule and calendar

Swipe between days, or tap the date to choose a day from the monthly calendar. Class times, rooms, and personal notes stay visible in the daily view.

<img src="docs/01-day.jpg" width="280" alt="Daily timetable with class times, rooms, and a personal consultation"> <img src="docs/15-calendar.jpg" width="280" alt="Monthly calendar for jumping to a specific date">

## Exams and personal events

**Ispiti i provjere** brings upcoming exams and assessments together, ordered by the nearest date, with times, locations, and a shortcut back to the timetable. Add your own events with a colour and note, and optionally include them in the assessment list. Long-press a class to save a personal change to its date, time, or room.

<img src="docs/19-upcoming-exams.png" width="280" alt="Upcoming exams and assessments with dates, countdowns, times, and locations"> <img src="docs/21-add-to-assessments.png" width="280" alt="Personal event editor with notes and an option to include the event in assessments">

## Make the timetable yours

Choose your LV group separately for each subject and switch between light and dark themes. Successfully downloaded weeks remain available offline, together with your personal events and changes.

<img src="docs/03-lv-groups.jpg" width="280" alt="Subject-by-subject laboratory group selection"> <img src="docs/12-dark-mode.jpg" width="280" alt="Daily timetable in dark mode">

Screenshots show example personal events and timetable changes, which are saved on the device and do not change the official FERIT timetable.

## Install on Android

[Download APK — version 1.0.14](https://github.com/KTihana/FERIT-raspored/releases/download/v1.0.14-android-test/FERIT-raspored.apk) · Android 7.0+

Open the downloaded file and install it. Install over the previous app to keep your saved choices. The app runs locally; internet is needed for FERIT timetable data and update checks.

## Updates

When **Dostupno ažuriranje** appears, tap **Preuzmi** and confirm installation on Android. Manual check: **Postavke → Ažuriranja aplikacije → Provjeri ažuriranja**.

Restore the original timetable: **Postavke → Vrati izvorni raspored**.

[Source project](https://github.com/KTihana/FERIT-raspored/releases/download/v1.0.14-android-test/FERIT-raspored-source.zip) · iPhone installation requires Xcode and Apple signing.
